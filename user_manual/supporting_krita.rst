================
Supporting Krita
================

Krita is free, you don't need to purchase anything to use it. If you enjoy Krita and want to support its continued development or work in a company that relies on it, there are ways to support it though.

Donations
=========

Donating to Krita is the way that most of your money actually arrives. It has the least amount of fees and less taxes.

You can make regular, repeating donations via `the Krita Development Fund <https://fund.krita.org/>`__. There is also an option for corporations and other organizations to sponser Krita this way.

For one-time donations, take a look at `the donation page on krita.org <https://krita.org/en/donations/>`__. You can donate via credit card, PayPal, bank transfer and other regional options.

Purchases
=========

You can buy Krita on various online stores:

* `Krita on Steam <https://store.steampowered.com/app/280680/Krita/>`__
* `Krita on the Epic Games Store <https://store.epicgames.com/p/krita>`__
* `Krita on the Microsoft Store <https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/store/p/krita/9n6x57zgrw96>`__
* `Krita on the App Store for Mac <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/krita/id1594607976?mt=12>`__

If you purchase one these versions, you get auto-updates via the store you purchased it from. Steam also gives you some cloud storage. The program itself is the same, there are no features locked behind a paywall.

You can also purchase some resource packs, training materials and more `on the shop page on krita.org <https://krita.org/en/shop/>`__.

Commercial Support
==================

If your company or studio needs dedicated support or wants to sponsor the development features or improvements to Krita, take a look at `the section on development contracting on krita.org <https://krita.org/en/donations/#development-contracting>`__
